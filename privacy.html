Official Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 31, 2026  |  Application: Sprout (Package: com.siamsoftlabs.sprout)

1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to Sprout (referred to as "the App", "we", "us", or "our"), developed by Siam Labs. Your privacy is foundational to our philosophy. Sprout is designed as an offline-first focus and habit tracker that helps you stay mindful without tracking your private life.

🔒 Summary: We do not sell your personal data. We do not use third-party ad networks. We do not track your activity across other apps or websites. Your habit data stays on your device.

2. Information We Process

A. Local App Data (Stored Strictly on Your Device)

Sprout stores your timer sessions, focus history, tree/plant progress, and app preferences locally on your device via SQLite. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third-party analytics services.

B. Subscription & Financial Data

If you choose to subscribe to Sprout Plus (Monthly or Annual subscription):

  • Payment Processing: All financial transactions are managed and processed directly by Google Play Billing. We never receive, process, or store your credit card numbers or banking credentials.
  • Purchase Validation: We use RevenueCat to manage subscription entitlements. When a purchase occurs, pseudonymous receipt data (such as product ID, transaction timestamp, and an anonymous App User ID) is processed to verify your subscription status and unlock premium features.

C. Device & Technical Identifiers

To maintain active subscription state and manage purchase validation, RevenueCat collects pseudonymous identifiers (e.g., anonymous RevenueCat user ID, device hardware model, and OS version). These identifiers are used solely for the functionality of subscription entitlements.

D. Account Information

Sprout does not require an account creation or registration. You can use all core features anonymously without providing an email address, name, or phone number.

3. How We Use Collected Information

The minimal technical data collected is used strictly for the following purposes:

  • App Functionality: Providing premium features, restoring active purchases, and validating subscription status across app reinstalls.
  • Customer Support: Responding to user-initiated queries and troubleshooting purchase issues when you contact us directly.
  • Legal & Compliance: Fulfilling obligations related to app store billing rules and applicable regulations.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted industry providers to process billing and manage subscriptions securely:

  • Google Play Services / Google Play In-App Billing (Google LLC):
    Handles payment processing, store presence, and distribution.
    Google Privacy Policy →
  • RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.):
    Manages subscription infrastructure, entitlement verification, and receipt validation.
    RevenueCat Privacy Policy →

5. Data Security & Retention

Encryption in Transit: All communications between the App, Google Play Billing, and RevenueCat are encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).

Data Retention: Local habit data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app data. Purchase records are retained in Google Play and RevenueCat only as long as necessary to administer your active subscription or comply with legal requirements.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You maintain complete control over your data:

  • Delete Local Data: You can wipe all local focus and habit records at any time by clearing the application storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.
  • Data Inquiries & Deletion Requests: You may request information regarding your subscription verification data or ask for deletion of any associated anonymous identifiers by contacting us via email at support@siamsoftlabs.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Sprout does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal data, please contact us so we can take immediate corrective measures.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable laws. Any modifications will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
